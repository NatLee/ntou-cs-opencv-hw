Heart rate from skin colour heartbeats · FaceAndHeartbeatDetection

Skin gets a little redder with every heartbeat. Both programs average the red of skin pixels in one area and count its rises and falls.

heartbeats

red_thres = 0.0
FaceAndHeartbeatDetection
A Haar cascade finds the face (green ellipse). With exactly one face, its box is the area (orange).
red_thres = 0.35
Every frame: average red of the skin pixels in the area
Illustration. When the average rises after falling, or falls after rising, by more than red_thres, the program counts one flip (dots). Two flips are one beat, and small wiggles count too.
1 · Skin mask
YCrCb, Cr 137–177 and Cb 77–127, the same range as the neckline programs.
2 · Count flips
Rise after fall or fall after rise, compared with the previous frame only.
3 · Beats per minute
beats ÷ seconds × 60, drawn on the frame as “HeartBeat time: … per minutes” or “Rate: …”.
Seconds from clock(): that is the processor time the program used, not the time on the wall, so the rate depends on how busy the CPU is. With red_thres 0.0 any flicker counts.
Face version: the pixel loop stops at faces[0].y + width instead of x + width, and the text is drawn at (y, x). The eye cascade is loaded but never used.
