ntou-cs-opencv-hw

Seven OpenCV programs from 2016 that read text, count breaths and estimate a heart rate.

$docker build .
Text

Find characters and read them

TextDetectionwebcam

Press c: threshold the red box, cut out character-sized blobs, read each with a CNN classifier.

TextDetection4WaysTEST.jpg

Projections find the line and the digits; four quadrant ratios are matched to 0–9 templates.

TextDetectionUsingProfile7.jpg

Row and column projections split a shop receipt into lines and blocks.

Breathing

Watch the neckline move

NecklineDetectionUsingNormalMethodwebcam

Skin colour finds where the neck meets the collar; each up-and-down is one breath.

NecklineDetectionUsingOpticalFlowwebcam

Same collar point, plus optical flow arrows along it; prints up / down.

Heart rate

Follow the redness of skin

heartbeatswebcam

Average red of the biggest skin area; its rises and falls are counted as beats.

FaceAndHeartbeatDetectionwebcam

The same, but inside a face found by a Haar cascade.
